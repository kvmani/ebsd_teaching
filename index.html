What Students Should Notice

Gain brightens weak patterns, but excessive gain saturates bands and hides detail.

Binning combines camera pixels: cleaner signal, lower spatial detail.

Exposure collects more electrons per pixel: cleaner indexing, slower scan.

Averaging suppresses random noise, but the sample spends longer at each point.

Drift shifts the raster during acquisition, bending boundaries and lowering confidence.

Indexing depends on detecting enough bands above a confidence threshold.
